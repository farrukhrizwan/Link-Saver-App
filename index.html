Privacy Policy

100% Zero-Server Data Guarantee

Link Saver App operates strictly on an offline-first client architecture. All your saved web links, social links, crypto wallet recovery phrases, and passwords remain 100% inside your device's browser local storage (`localStorage`).

Encryption & Security

Sensitive information such as crypto recovery phrases and passwords are encrypted using Web Crypto API 256-bit AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation derived directly from your personal PIN.

No Analytics, No Tracking, No Cloud Sync

We do not collect telemetry, IP addresses, cookies, or personal identifier data. You retain total control over your encrypted local backups.

Local Data & Backup Responsibility

Because Link Saver App stores all data locally in your browser without sending any copy to external servers, you are solely responsible for keeping backups of your PIN and data. Clearing your browser storage without a JSON backup will erase local records.
